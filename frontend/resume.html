How to Create a Resume (Step by Step)

1. Personal Information
Add your Full Name, Phone Number and Email Address.

2. Career Objective
Write a short statement about your career goals.

3. Education
Mention your degree, college name, and CGPA or percentage.

4. Technical Skills
Include programming languages and tools like Java, Python, HTML, SQL.

5. Projects
Explain your academic or personal projects with technologies used.

6. Internships
Mention internships and what skills you gained.

7. Certifications
Add certifications from platforms like Coursera, NPTEL, Udemy.

8. Achievements
Add awards, competitions or hackathons.

9. Soft Skills
Mention teamwork, leadership, communication skills.

10. Keep Resume Short
For freshers the resume should be 1 page.
